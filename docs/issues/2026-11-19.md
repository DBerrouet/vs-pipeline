# Vegan Startup Weekly — draft 2026-11-19

**Subject:** Sunfeast launches fibre-rich plant-based nutrition shake in India, and Forager Project lands senior debt from SG Credit Partners  
**Preview:** Plus: Danone completes takeover of Cocobella owner Made Group, Freestyle Snacks secures US$5M seed round, Symbiotec to build precision fermentation capacity for European client.

### The big one: Sunfeast launches fibre-rich plant-based nutrition shake in India

Indian conglomerate ITC Foods has introduced a new dairy-free shake under its Sunfeast brand. The product combines plant-based and yeast proteins with high fibre content. The launch comes amid growing corporate interest in smart proteins in India. ([Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/itc-foods-sunfeast-protein-fibre-plant-based-yeast-nutrition-shake-india/))

**Why it matters:** A large incumbent entering plant-based nutrition signals rising competition and validation for vegan protein startups in India. Vegan status of the shake is not confirmed in the excerpt, as it is described as dairy-free rather than explicitly vegan.

### Launch radar

- **Meliora Foods (Australia):** launched a pourable plant-based egg alternative made from mung beans, targeting the breakfast market as Australian egg consumption hits record highs. ([Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/meliora-plant-based-egg-vegan-liquid-mung-beans-replacer-substitute-australia/))
- **Meliora (My Co) (Australia):** launched a ready-to-pour plant-based liquid egg in Coles, made with mung bean protein isolate from sister company Australian Plant Proteins. ([vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/food-and-beverage/egg-alternatives/meliora-liquid-egg-enters-coles-using-mung-bean-isolate-sister-company-australian-plant-proteins/))
- **Táche:** debuts Peppermint Mocha Barista, its first chocolate pistachio milk, as a limited-edition plant-based drink. ([The PlantBase Mag](https://www.theplantbasemag.com/post301/t%C3%A1che-launches-first-chocolate-pistachio-milk-with-limited-edition-peppermint-mocha-barista))
- **Alghemie (Italy):** makes Perle di Sirena, a seaweed-based plant caviar produced in a small Treviso workshop, and is pushing it through sushi competitions and cooking events. ([vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/algae-microalgae-seaweed/inside-alghemie-treviso-brand-turning-seaweed-into-plant-based-caviar/))

### Regulation watch

- **New Culture (United States):** received California approval for its precision-fermentation mozzarella manufacturing process, facility and label, with the product allowed to be called "Mozzarella Style Animal-free Dairy Cheese". ([vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/fermentation/new-culture-secures-california-approval-produce-animal-free-dairy-mozzarella/) · [Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/new-culture-california-cdfa-approval-precision-fermentation-casein-mozzarella-cheese/) · [AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/a-massive-step-closer-to-commercialization-new-culture-gets-ok-to-make-animal-free-dairy-cheese-in-california))
- **All G (Australia):** gained self-affirmed GRAS status in the US for its precision-fermented human lactoferrin, the first in a planned breast milk protein pipeline. ([Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/all-g-human-lactoferrin-precision-fermentation-us-gras-recombinant-protein/))
- **21st.Bio (Denmark):** filed an EU regulatory dossier for BLG+ Essential, its precision-fermented beta-lactoglobulin, and is pushing for a faster approval pathway. ([Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/21st-bio-precision-fermentation-beta-lactoglobulin-eu-approval-efsa-novel-food/) · [The PlantBase Mag](https://www.theplantbasemag.com/post301/21st-bio-files-eu-novel-food-application-for-precision-fermented-beta-lactoglobulin))

### Exits and shutdowns

- **AM Nutrition (Norway):** pea and faba bean protein ingredient processor is winding down, affecting 27 jobs in Norway and Denmark, as parent cooperative FKRA cites weak margins. ([vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/company-news/norways-am-nutrition-closes-after-19-years-pea-fractionation/))
