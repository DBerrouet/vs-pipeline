The big one: Sunfeast launches fibre-rich plant-based nutrition shake in India

Indian conglomerate ITC Foods has introduced a new dairy-free shake under its Sunfeast brand. The product combines plant-based and yeast proteins with high fibre content. The launch comes amid growing corporate interest in smart proteins in India. (Green Queen)

Why it matters: A large incumbent entering plant-based nutrition signals rising competition and validation for vegan protein startups in India. Vegan status of the shake is not confirmed in the excerpt, as it is described as dairy-free rather than explicitly vegan.

Money moves

Launch radar

  • Meliora Foods (Australia): launched a pourable plant-based egg alternative made from mung beans, targeting the breakfast market as Australian egg consumption hits record highs. (Green Queen)

  • Meliora (My Co) (Australia): launched a ready-to-pour plant-based liquid egg in Coles, made with mung bean protein isolate from sister company Australian Plant Proteins. (vegconomist)

  • Táche: debuts Peppermint Mocha Barista, its first chocolate pistachio milk, as a limited-edition plant-based drink. (The PlantBase Mag)

  • Alghemie (Italy): makes Perle di Sirena, a seaweed-based plant caviar produced in a small Treviso workshop, and is pushing it through sushi competitions and cooking events. (vegconomist)

Regulation watch

  • New Culture (United States): received California approval for its precision-fermentation mozzarella manufacturing process, facility and label, with the product allowed to be called "Mozzarella Style Animal-free Dairy Cheese". (vegconomist · Green Queen · AgFunderNews)

  • All G (Australia): gained self-affirmed GRAS status in the US for its precision-fermented human lactoferrin, the first in a planned breast milk protein pipeline. (Green Queen)

  • 21st.Bio (Denmark): filed an EU regulatory dossier for BLG+ Essential, its precision-fermented beta-lactoglobulin, and is pushing for a faster approval pathway. (Green Queen · The PlantBase Mag)

Exits and shutdowns
